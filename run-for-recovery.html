About the Event

What Is
Run for
Recovery?

Run for Recovery is our 8th annual community 5K — a Thanksgiving-morning run or walk that brings the Phoenix recovery community together.

Every registration raises money to keep Barbell Saves Project programs free for people in recovery. No experience needed. Runners, walkers, and families all welcome.

Join us November 26th at 8 AM at 11042 N 24th Ave, Suite 104, for a morning of movement, community, and gratitude before the holiday.
